Theme Overview

Edge of Becoming

There is a space between who we are and who we are becoming — a space filled with questions, possibilities, uncertainty and discovery.

Edge of Becoming invited us to explore that space. Through stories, ideas and perspectives from diverse voices, TEDxTNS Youth 2026 encouraged us to look beyond the familiar, question what we know, and imagine what might be possible. It was a celebration of curiosity, courage and the many ways we continue to grow, change and become.

A Day of Ideas, Imagination & Possibility

TEDxTNS Youth 2026 brought together ideas, conversations, technology and experiences across a day designed to spark curiosity and possibility.
